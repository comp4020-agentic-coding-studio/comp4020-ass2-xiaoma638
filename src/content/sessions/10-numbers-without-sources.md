In week 1 you stood outside an interface you did not build and asked what could
possibly support what it said. Today you do it to your own, and the exercise is
not the same one.

## What changed since week 1

In week 1 you had a recording. Today you have the source, the state enumeration
you wrote in [week 2](/sessions/02-what-counts-as-done/), the estimator you
chose in [week 5](/sessions/05-why-the-estimate-jumps/) and the instability
numbers you measured for it, and every failure state you can reproduce on
demand.

So the question sharpens. It is no longer *what could support this claim?* It is
**which of my promises actually hold?** And the verdicts change with it:

| Week 1 | Week 10 |
| --- | --- |
| `sourced` — some event could support it | `holds` — **this** event produces it, and you can name the line |
| `unsourceable` — nothing could | `removed` — nothing does, so it is gone from the screen |
| `not determinable from outside` | *unavailable to you* |

That third row is the point of the week. You are inside now. "I cannot tell" is
no longer an answer you are entitled to, and the discomfort of losing it is the
exercise working.

What replaces it is a fourth verdict week 1 had no use for: **kept with
reason**. Some claim on your screen has no source event, you know it, and you
are keeping it anyway — a rounded figure, a minimum display duration, a
reassuring phrase during verification. That can be a perfectly good decision.
It stops being one the moment it is unexamined.

## What you build

A **claims register** for your own prototype, as a page in your repo. It is a
deliverable of [Assignment 3](/assessments/honest-waiting-kit/), so build it
where it will ship.

A row scores as "kept with reason" when the reason is something a reader could
argue with. "It looks better" is not a reason. "The verification step completes
in under 80 ms and a state that flashes is harder to read than one held for
400 ms, so this state is displayed for longer than it exists" is a reason, and a
marker may still disagree with it.

## What this week cannot tell you

Whether the claims that survive are *understood*. You have established that
every number on your screen has an event behind it. You have established
nothing about whether anybody reading it takes from it what you meant. That is
[week 11](/sessions/11-watching-someone-wait/), and it will find things this
register cannot — including, quite possibly, a claim that holds perfectly and
still misleads.
