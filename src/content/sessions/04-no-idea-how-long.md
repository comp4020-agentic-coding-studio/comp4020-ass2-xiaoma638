Tuesday's lecture,
[Saying Nothing, Precisely](/lectures/week-04/), separates *not knowing how long*
from *having nothing to say*. Today you build the three registers it names.

## In the room

Thursday 18 March, 14:00–17:00. Build session; the teaching was Tuesday.

## What you build

Three treatments against an upload whose size is never revealed: **liveness**
(something is still happening), **elapsed** (how long you have waited), and
**named stage** (which thing is happening now, out of an unknown total). Plus
one page of rules for when each is allowed.

The hard constraint: nothing may admit a fractional reading. A spinner that
slows down is claiming to be near the end.

## What this week cannot tell you

Whether users prefer any of them. That is [week 6](/sessions/06-motion-and-the-felt-wait/),
and the answer will be less conclusive than you expect.
