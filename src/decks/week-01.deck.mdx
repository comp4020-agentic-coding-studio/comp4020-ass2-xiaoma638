# What 99% promises

SLOP2805 · Week 1

{/* _class: quote */}

## It sat at 99% for eleven seconds

Then it said the upload failed.

Which of those two things was the lie?

---

## A claim is anything the interface asserts

- a **number** — "99%", "about 4 minutes remaining"
- a **word** — "Finalising", "Almost done", "Complete"
- a **motion** — a bar that accelerates is claiming something about rate
- an **absence** — a screen showing nothing claims nothing needs saying

If a reader can take something from it, it is a claim.

---

## A source event is something the process *reports*

Not something it does. Something it emits, at a time you could write down.

| the process does | the process reports |
| --- | --- |
| writes bytes to a socket | `progress: 4.2 MB of 11 MB` |
| waits on a server lock | *(nothing)* |
| finishes durably | `committed` |

The middle row is where progress bars are invented.

---

## Three verdicts, this week only

- `sourced` — an event exists that could support it
- `unsourceable` — nothing the process emits could support it
- `not determinable from outside` — you are out here, and you cannot tell

In **week 10** you do this to your own code, and the third verdict is taken
away.
