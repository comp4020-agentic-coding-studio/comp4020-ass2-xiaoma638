# Saying nothing, precisely

SLOP2805 · Week 4 · Lecture

There is no total. There will not be one. The interface still has to
show something.

---

## Where we were, and where we are going

**Week 3** established that a denominator is a countable source event, and that
the platform tells you when one exists.

**This week** is the case where it does not: `lengthComputable === false`.

By the end you can:

- separate *not knowing the duration* from *having nothing to say*
- build liveness, elapsed and named-stage feedback
- spot a fractional reading in a display containing no numbers

---

## Three honest registers

| Register | Asserts | Needs | Says nothing about |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Liveness** | something is still happening | a heartbeat event | position, duration, success |
| **Elapsed** | you have waited this long | a clock | the process at all |
| **Named stage** | *this* is happening now | the week 2 model | how many remain |

---

## Elapsed is the one people skip

It is the only register that **cannot be wrong**. It is a fact about the user,
not a prediction about the process.

It also does something no error handler does:

> A counter reading **0:45** on a process that usually takes **0:06** has
> communicated a problem — with no stall detection written at all.

---

## The fractional-reading trap

An indeterminate display must not admit a **ratio**. The test is what a reader
can take, not what the code intends.

- a spinner that **slows down** → "nearly there"
- three dots, on the third → "two-thirds"
- a bar that **sweeps** left to right → position
- "Step 2" with no total → readers estimate the total from the ordinal

If a user can form a fraction from it, you made a determinate claim with
indeterminate data.

---

## Worked example — same run, three treatments

Simulated unknown-length stream, `upload-ui` with `lengthComputable = false`:

```
liveness      ◆ ◆ ◆ ◆            "Still uploading"
elapsed       0:12               "Uploading — 12 seconds so far"
named stage   ▣ read ▣ hash ▶ transfer    "Transferring"
```

All three are true at t = 12 s. None of them implies a fraction.

The fourth thing most people build — a bar creeping to 90% and waiting — implies
one, and has no event behind it.

```comment
The stream length is withheld by the shipped simulator; it is not a real transfer.
```

## The mistake to expect

Reaching for `value="0"` when there is no value.

```html
<div role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="0">        <!-- ✗ "nothing has happened" -->
<div role="progressbar" aria-valuetext="Transferring, total unknown">
                                                   <!-- ✓ indeterminate -->
```

Omitting `aria-valuenow` is how the platform spells *indeterminate*. Setting it
to zero is a different claim, and usually a false one. This is audited in
**week 7**.

You have built three truthful displays. Truthful is not comprehensible, and
