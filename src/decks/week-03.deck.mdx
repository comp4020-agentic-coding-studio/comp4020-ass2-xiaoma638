# Where is the denominator?

SLOP2805 · Week 3 · Studio framing

When your bar says 40%, forty per cent of **what** has happened?

---

## Where we were, and where we are going

**Week 2** gave you states. **This week** turns them into a number — and finds
out what the number leaves out.

By 17:00 you can:

- name the denominator behind any percentage, or show there isn't one
- measure where two honest denominators disagree, on the same run
- say in one sentence what your chosen denominator excludes

---

## Back to the case from week 1

40 MB. Bar reaches 99% in nine seconds. Then eleven seconds of nothing.

**One ordinary explanation:** the bar was counting bytes handed to the network
stack. All 40 MB went into a buffer in nine seconds. The eleven seconds were the
server hashing and writing.

The bar was not lying. It was answering a different question from the one the
user was asking.

---

## Two denominators, both honest

**Byte-counted** — `loaded / total`, straight from the platform.
Cheap, smooth, frequent. Excludes everything that is not transfer.

**Stage-counted** — `stagesDone / stagesTotal`, from your week 2 model.
Covers the whole process. Moves in lurches, and assumes all stages are the same
size, which they never are.

**Weighted stages** are honest only if the weights were measured. A guessed
weight is a number with no source event.

---

## The platform is blunt about this

```js
xhr.upload.addEventListener("progress", (e) => {
  if (!e.lengthComputable) return showIndeterminate();  // no denominator
  setPercent((e.loaded / e.total) * 100);
});
```

`lengthComputable` is the platform telling you whether a denominator exists at
all. Most code reads `e.total` without checking.

That flag is next week.

---

## Worked example — where they part company

Simulated run, `trace-office-wifi.json`, both readings on one timeline:

| t | bytes | stages | gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 s | 31% | 25% | 6 |
| 9 s | 74% | 50% | 24 |
| 14 s | **99%** | 50% | **49** |
| 21 s | 99% | 75% | 24 |
| 32 s | 100% | 100% | 0 |

Largest disagreement: **49 points, at t = 14 s** — the moment the bytes finished
and the verification had not started reporting.

## The mistake to expect

Writing the exclusion as a category instead of a consequence:

> ✗ "Shows upload progress."
>
> ✗ "Percentage of transfer."
>
> ✓ "Bytes sent. Does not include the server-side check, which on this trace
> took a further eleven seconds."

The test: could a waiting user predict the 99% pause from your sentence? If not,
the sentence is decoration.

---

## What this week cannot tell you

Which denominator is **right**.

Nothing measured today settles that, because "right" depends on what the user is
waiting to find out, and nobody has asked one. You now know what each choice
omits and what that omission is worth in points — on one recorded trace.

The first outside reaction to your choice is **week 11**.
