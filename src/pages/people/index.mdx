Two people teach SLOP2805. Between them they are in the room for six of the
fifteen contact hours a fortnight, which is unusual for a course this size and
is the reason the studios are three hours rather than two.

## Where to find us

**In the studio, first.** Thursdays, 14:00&ndash;17:00. That is what three hours
with two staff is for, and it is the fastest route to an answer on every week of
this course &mdash; most of what looks like a conceptual problem turns out, at
the machine, to be a state that was never named.

**Email**, for anything that cannot wait until Thursday. Addresses are on the
pages below.

**Not by appointment.** There is no separate consultation hour, deliberately:
the studio is long enough that the conversation can happen while the code is on
the screen, which is where it is useful.

## What to bring

The thing that is broken, rather than a description of it. A running prototype
in a state you can reproduce is worth twenty minutes of explanation, and the
[progress simulator](/simulator/) is there partly so you can point at the
behaviour you mean.

If you are behind, say so early. Every week has a stage checkpoint precisely so
that an unfinished week does not cost you the next one, and using it is a
normal thing to do rather than an admission.

## Who teaches what

The four [lectures](/lectures/) are the convenor's. The studios are shared, and
the [calendar](/calendar/) lists who is in the room each week &mdash; it is read
from the same records the week pages are, so it is the version to trust.
